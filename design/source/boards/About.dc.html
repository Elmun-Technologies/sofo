sofo tells you where a movie or series is streaming in your country — and whether you can watch it dubbed or subtitled in your language. It’s free, and it works with the services you already pay for.

1You searchA title, an actor, or just “anime with Turkish dub”.
2We check the servicesCatalogs, prices, audio and subtitle languages — every day, for your country.
3You watch thereOne tap opens the title on the official service. We never host video.
Where our data comes fromTitles, posters and descriptions: TMDB. Availability, prices and languages: the services’ own catalogs, checked daily. Every page shows when it was last checked.
What we never doWe don’t host or stream video. We don’t link to pirate sites. If a service doesn’t confirm a language, we don’t show it.
How sofo makes moneyToday: it doesn’t. Later, some links may be affiliate links — they never change the order or what we show.
