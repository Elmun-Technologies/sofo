About Turkish dub on streaming

Dublaj or altyazı — what’s the difference?

Dublaj means the voices are in Turkish. Altyazı means original voices with Turkish text on screen. We show both separately for every service.

How do you know a title has Turkish audio?

We read the audio and subtitle lists the services publish for Türkiye and check them every day. If a service doesn’t confirm it, we don’t show it.

Why is a dub on Netflix but not on Prime?

Each service licenses its own audio tracks. The same movie can have Turkish audio on one service and only subtitles on another.
